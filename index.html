About Us

Morazha Co-operative Arts & Science College is an institution providing higher education in arts, science and computer-related programmes.

The college provides students with opportunities for academic learning, co-curricular activities and overall development.

  • Well equipped laboratories
  • Qualified and experienced teachers
  • Library with good collection of books
  • Sports and games facilities
